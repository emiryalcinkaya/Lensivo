A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your first take.

What happened to FaceView Preview?

Lensivo is the next chapter of FaceView Preview. The camera preview remains, with screen recording tools joining in version 2.0. The App Store link still points to the existing app listing.

Does this website access my camera?

No. The interactive demo is an illustration of the controls. It does not record, request permissions, or access your camera, microphone, or screen.

Where do my recordings go?

Recordings are written locally, then saved to the folder you choose. Interrupted or unsaved recordings may remain in the app’s recovery area. You can inspect, export, or delete them from Settings.

Which permissions does Lensivo need?

Camera access is used for preview and facecam. Microphone access is used when microphone recording is enabled. Screen and system audio permissions depend on the capture source you choose. You stay in control in macOS System Settings.
